Tablet Kiosk & Guided Access Setup

Clinical Lock-in for Apple iPad & Android Tablets

🍎 Apple iPad Guided Access (Clinical Standard):
  1. Open iPad Settings ➔ Accessibility ➔ Guided Access.
  2. Toggle Guided Access ON and set a Passcode.
  3. Launch Amina AAC in Safari or from your Home Screen.
  4. Triple-click the Top Power button (or Home button) to start Guided Access!
  5. Result: The communicator cannot swipe out to YouTube, games, or close the app.
🤖 Android Tablet App Pinning (Samsung Galaxy Tab / Fire HD / Lenovo):
  1. Open Android Settings ➔ Security (or Biometrics) ➔ App Pinning.
  2. Toggle App Pinning ON.
  3. Open Amina AAC ➔ Swipe up to Recent Apps ➔ Tap the Amina app icon ➔ Select "Pin".
  4. Result: The navigation bar is locked until the caregiver unpins with a PIN.

💡 How to install authentic African & UK voices on your device

• Windows 10/11: Settings ➔ Time & Language ➔ Speech ➔ Add voices ➔ Search "English (United Kingdom)" or "English (Nigeria)" or "English (South Africa)".

• Android / Chromebook: Settings ➔ Accessibility ➔ Text-to-speech output ➔ Install voice data ➔ English (Nigeria / UK / South Africa).

• iPad & iPhone: Settings ➔ Accessibility ➔ Spoken Content ➔ Voices ➔ English ➔ Download Serena, Stephanie, or Tessa.

Quick Guide: Amina in 30 Seconds

Everything you need to know for toddlers, kids, and adults

💬
1. Build Sentences & Speak

Tap colored symbols in order (e.g. I ➔ WANT ➔ EAT ➔ APPLE), then tap the green SPEAK button! Use +S, +ING, or +ED to expand grammar.

↩️
2. Accidental Clear Protection

Did a child accidentally tap 🗑️ Clear? An ↩️ Undo button appears automatically so you can restore their sentence with a single tap!

⭐
3. Star Favorite Phrases

Build a phrase you use all the time (like "I need water please") and tap the golden ⭐ Favorite button. It keeps it ready at the top forever!

🔤
4. Phonics & Food Decision Wheels

Switch tabs anytime to practice reading on the Hooked on Phonics blending board, or spin the Food Wheels when a child is stuck deciding what to eat!

📱
5. Clinical Tablet Access & Keyguards

Have a child with tremors or cerebral palsy? Open ⚙️ Settings to enable Touch on Release, Hold Duration (Dwell), or Deep Keyguard Insets.
