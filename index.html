💡 How to install authentic African & UK voices on your device

• Windows 10/11: Settings ➔ Time & Language ➔ Speech ➔ Add voices ➔ Search "English (United Kingdom)" or "English (Nigeria)" or "English (South Africa)".

• Android / Chromebook: Settings ➔ Accessibility ➔ Text-to-speech output ➔ Install voice data ➔ English (Nigeria / UK / South Africa).

• iPad & iPhone: Settings ➔ Accessibility ➔ Spoken Content ➔ Voices ➔ English ➔ Download Serena, Stephanie, or Tessa.
